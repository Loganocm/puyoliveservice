## Context

What forces the decision. The problem, the constraints, what was measured.
Write it so a reader who was not there understands why doing nothing was not
an option.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. Include what was considered and rejected, and
why, when that is not obvious.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what is now a rule, and what must be
done as a result (goldens, ENGINE_VERSION, migrations, docs).
