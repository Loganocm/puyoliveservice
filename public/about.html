About the Game

What is Puyo Live?

Puyo Live is a free, browser-based Puyo Puyo alternative built from the ground up for competitive online play. No downloads, no installs — just open your browser and start chaining.

Whether you're a newcomer learning to build your first chain or a seasoned competitive stacker grinding ranked matches, Puyo Live delivers the fast-paced match-4 puzzle action you love with modern performance and real-time multiplayer.

Features

Real-Time Multiplayer

Play Puyo online against opponents from around the world with low-latency WebSocket connections. Jump into ranked matches for Elo-rated competition, or create custom rooms to play with friends.

Classic Chaining Mechanics

Puyo Live faithfully recreates the chain-building puzzle gameplay that fans love. Master advanced techniques like:

  • GTR (Great Tanaka Rensa) — the foundational competitive chain pattern
  • Stairs — a beginner-friendly staircase chain to build consistent combos
  • Sandwich Chaining — an advanced technique for maximum garbage output

Build massive chains, send devastating garbage, and outplay your opponents with superior stacking strategy.

Competitive Rankings

Climb the global leaderboard with Elo-based matchmaking. Track your stats, review your match history, and prove you're the best competitive stacker on the web.

Single Player Modes

Warm up with timed challenges — 3-minute, 5-minute, or 10-minute time attacks to practice your chains and improve your score before heading into multiplayer.

Built for the Browser

Puyo Live runs entirely in your web browser with no plugins or downloads required. Built with modern web technologies for smooth 60fps gameplay on any device. Play the premier browser Puyo experience anywhere, anytime.
